# Pre-Mortem Prompt

You are conducting a pre-mortem analysis for a potential investment position.

**Asset:** {{ASSET_NAME}} ({{SYMBOL}})
**Proposed action:** {{ACTION}} at {{ENTRY_PRICE}}
**Time horizon:** {{TIME_HORIZON}}
**Thesis:** {{THESIS_SUMMARY}}

Assume this investment loses 50% over the next 12 months.

## Questions to answer

1. **Most likely failure causes**
   List the 3-5 most plausible scenarios that produce a -50% outcome. Be specific — not "market downturn" but "BTC falls below $40k, triggering liquidation cascades that take high-beta alts down 60-80%."

2. **Warning signs visible today**
   What signals are already present that could be early indicators of this failure? Be honest about what you might be dismissing.

3. **Weekly monitoring checklist**
   What specific data points should be tracked weekly to catch this early?
   - For crypto: on-chain metrics, funding rates, liquidation levels, whale flows, unlock schedule
   - For equity: revenue guidance revisions, margin compression, insider selling, debt covenants

4. **Thesis invalidation triggers**
   What single event or threshold would prove the thesis is broken?
   (Not just a price level — a fundamental change in the investment case.)

5. **Exit criteria**
   If the thesis is invalidating, what is the exit trigger?
   - Thesis stop: [specific condition]
   - Price stop: [specific level]
   - Time stop: [date or period]

Be adversarial. The purpose of this analysis is to find reasons NOT to make this investment, not to validate it.
